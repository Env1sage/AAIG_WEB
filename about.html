About AAIG

Protecting Production. Delivering Certainty.

AA Infinite Global Alliance (AAIG) was founded on a single insight: that the gap between what Tier-1 suppliers are capable of and what global OEMs consistently receive is an execution problem — not a capability problem. We exist to close that gap.

Our Founding Story

Built From a Defining Insight

After spending close to three decades in international automotive and manufacturing, Anand realized something profound — India's Tier-1 industry had the capability, but not always the consistency that global OEMs depend on. The failures weren't technical. They were operational: containment that arrived too late, logistics that couldn't respond fast enough, quality systems that lacked OEM-aligned discipline.

That insight became the turning point. AAIG was founded not to add another service layer, but to become the execution partner that Tier-1 suppliers need when OEM relationships are on the line. Every capability AAIG offers — warehouse operations, expedited logistics, quality engineering, rework, resident engineering, launch support, and global representation — was designed to address the exact failure points that cost suppliers their most important customer relationships.

  • Rapid containment and quality fire-walling
  • Expedited logistics and 3PL for time-critical programs
  • OEM-aligned quality engineering and inspection
  • Technical rework and production recovery
  • Resident engineering and on-site program representation

Our Mission

To be the trusted execution partner for Tier-1 automotive suppliers — delivering technical, quality, and operational support that protects OEM relationships and sustains global production continuity.

Leadership & Vision

Experienced automotive quality professionals with deep industry knowledge

Anandkumar Abbad, CSCP

Founder & CEO

After spending close to three decades in international automotive and manufacturing — including senior leadership at Magna India's Exterior Division — Anand recognized a consistent pattern: Tier-1 suppliers had the engineering capability, but lacked the operational discipline and embedded execution support that global OEMs demand. That insight became the founding principle of AAIG.

His direct experience managing cross-border supply chains, leading product launches, and navigating high-stakes containment programs gave him a precise understanding of where suppliers fail — and exactly what needs to be in place to prevent it. AAIG was built as the answer to that problem.

  • 30+ years international automotive & manufacturing experience
  • Certified Supply Chain Professional (CSCP) – APICS (valid through Aug 2030)
  • Former Country Head – Magna India Exterior Division
  • Expert in OEM-aligned quality systems and global operations
  • Led multiple successful product launches and high-risk containment programs

Quality is not an act — it is a disciplined system. At AAIG, we build that system into every engagement so our clients never have to defend their OEM relationships.
