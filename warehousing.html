Managed Warehouse & Operational Transition Solutions

Structured Execution. Controlled Transition. Scalable Deployment.

AAIG develops and manages warehouse environments aligned with Tier-1 automotive and industrial program requirements. Whether stabilizing an existing operation, transitioning into a newly structured environment, or deploying a new warehouse under defined program scope — we ensure disciplined governance, cost-effective execution, and delivery continuity.

Structured Transition Models

Three Pathways to Operational Control

AAIG provides structured transition support across three deployment models — each designed to protect service continuity and align with Tier-1 program requirements.

Option 1: Existing Warehouse Stabilization

Where infrastructure is established, AAIG strengthens operational control through SLA and KPI alignment, containment & quality structuring, EDI / ASN integration, governance & escalation protocols, and cost-control optimization.

Objective: Enhance performance without disruption.

Option 2: Transition to New Environment

When growth, geographic alignment, or compliance requires infrastructure realignment, AAIG manages a phased transition including program-based footprint design, right-fit infrastructure sourcing, phased migration planning, governance & reporting framework, and controlled ramp-up.

Objective: Establish scalable, Tier-1 aligned operations with service continuity protected.

New Warehouse Deployment & RFQ Support

For new program launches or overseas expansion: right-sized footprint deployment, OEM proximity alignment, compliance & security structuring, and integrated quality & logistics capability.

Infrastructure scales with program demand — not speculation.
